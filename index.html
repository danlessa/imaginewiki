            About imagineWiki

            imagineWiki is an open take on the imagineRio atlas. Everything on the map comes from open, community-maintained projects:

            • OpenHistoricalMap: streets, buildings, rail lines and coastlines, filtered to the selected year.
            • Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons: historical photographs with a camera position, from Wikidata items (such as the Instituto Moreira Salles collection for Rio) and geotagged Commons files, and dated landmarks.
            • Wikimaps Warper: historical maps from Commons, georeferenced by volunteers.
            • GeoSampa (Prefeitura de São Paulo) and Pauliceia 2.0 (UNIFESP): georeferenced historical maps of São Paulo from 1868 to 1954.

            Something missing or wrong? Every source can be edited: add dates to features on OpenHistoricalMap, camera headings on Wikidata, or georeference another map on Warper.

            Inspired by imagineRio (Rice University and Axis Maps). This project is not affiliated with them.
